Our Story

Indo-Chinese comfort, the way Roha likes it.

The Noodle's Garden is a family Chinese restaurant on the Roha–Kolad road. Tables sit out in an open garden, strung with fairy lights, so dinner feels more like a get-together than a night out.

Our menu is built around what regulars ask for again and again: saucy noodles, smoky rice, juicy chicken starters and paneer for the vegetarians in the group.
